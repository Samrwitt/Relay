Transfer rooms

Share a six-character code or a link. Every device that opens it sits in the same encrypted room.

QR pairing

Flash the QR from your laptop. Your phone camera opens the room — no accounts, no app store.

Huge files, safely

Files are sliced into 64 KB chunks, encrypted with X25519 + XSalsa20-Poly1305, and acknowledged so a drop can resume.

Fallback relay

If ICE fails, chunks bounce through this server. The payload stays encrypted; the server only forwards bytes.
